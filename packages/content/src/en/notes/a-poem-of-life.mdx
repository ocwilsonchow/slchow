---
title: A Poem of Life
description: The moments that change a life rarely wait for us to be ready
date: 2026-08-12
---

A new calling.<br />
A leap into the unknown.<br />
A hand held for life.<br />
A city not yet learned—<br />

none of it begins with a plan.<br />

The moment comes.<br />
The blood brightens.<br />
You set your teeth,<br />
step through the door—<br />

and the whole world turns.<br />

Only the little things<br />
ask to be considered forever:<br />
what to eat,<br />
what to buy,<br />
where to spend an afternoon.<br />

I carried those words for years<br />
until living made them true.<br />

So, friends—<br />
do not linger at the threshold.<br />

The things that change a life<br />
rarely knock twice,<br />
and never wait<br />
for you to be ready.<br />
